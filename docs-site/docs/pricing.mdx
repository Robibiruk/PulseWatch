# Plans & Pricing

PulseWatch offers three tiers. Pay with Telegram Stars — no credit card required.

## Free

**$0/forever**

- 10 monitors
- 5-minute check interval
- Telegram alerts
- Basic incident history
- Public status page (1 theme)

## Pro — $5/month (50 Telegram Stars)

- 100 monitors
- 1-minute check interval
- Status pages (3 themes: neon, light, minimal)
- SSL certificate monitoring
- AI incident explanations (OpenRouter)
- Multi-channel alerts (Telegram + Email + Discord + Slack + Webhooks)
- API access

## Team — $15/month (150 Telegram Stars)

Everything in Pro, plus:

- Unlimited monitors
- 30-second check interval
- Team accounts
- Priority support

## How to Upgrade

1. Click **Upgrade** in the sidebar, or visit `/plan` in the dashboard
2. Select Pro or Team
3. Click the plan card — Telegram opens with the bot
4. Bot sends a Stars invoice — pay in Telegram
5. Plan is activated immediately after payment

## Trial

New accounts get a **14-day free trial** of Pro features. After the trial, you'll see an upgrade prompt on sign-in.

## Admin Access

The platform administrator (`robekmedia@gmail.com`) has permanent Team-tier access for development and testing.

## Payment FAQ

**Q: Do I need a credit card?**
A: No. Payments use Telegram Stars — users buy Stars through Apple/Google/Telegram and pay bots with them.

**Q: What happens after payment?**
A: The bot confirms your upgrade and you see the new plan in your dashboard immediately.

**Q: Can I downgrade?**
A: Contact support at robekmedia@gmail.com.

**Q: Is there a refund policy?**
A: Telegram Stars payments follow Telegram's refund policy. Contact support for assistance.
