# Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026

PulseWatch respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we use it.

## Data We Collect

**Account data:** Email, name, and notification preferences when you create an account.

**Monitor data:** URLs, check intervals, and configuration for each monitor you create.

**Check results:** Status codes, response times, timestamps, and incident metadata from every monitor check.

**Notification configuration:** Telegram chat IDs, email addresses, webhook URLs, and Discord/Slack webhook endpoints you provide.

## How We Use Your Data

- Run uptime checks and store results
- Send alerts through your configured channels
- Generate uptime reports and response time analytics
- Render public status pages you create
- Authenticate API requests

## What We Don't Do

- Sell your data to third parties
- Use monitoring data for advertising
- Track you across unrelated websites
- Collect unnecessary personal information

## Data Retention

- Check results: retained for 90 days on free plans, longer on paid tiers
- Incident timelines: retained indefinitely for audit purposes
- Account data: retained while your account is active

## Your Rights

You can export or delete your data at any time from the Settings page. For GDPR/CCPA requests, contact us via GitHub Issues.

## Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue at [github.com/Robibiruk/PulseWatch/issues](https://github.com/Robibiruk/PulseWatch/issues).
