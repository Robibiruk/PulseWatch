The PulseWatch Telegram bot turns chat messages into structured downtime alerts.

## Connecting

1. Open `@Pulse_WatchBot`
2. Press `Start`
3. Go to **Settings → Notifications → Connect Telegram**
4. Authorize the bot

Once connected, alerts go straight to Telegram: downtime, recovery, SSL expiry, heartbeat failure, and performance warnings.

## Testing

Use test notifications first. They simulate an incident without affecting uptime statistics or opening a real incident.

## Commands

- `/start`
- `/help`
- `/status`
- `/about`

Use `/status` for a quick health snapshot without leaving the chat.
